How to get started

Your first club session, step by step.

The club meet is informal. These simple pointers will help you know what to expect before you arrive.

01

Check the session details

We usually meet on Thursday at around 8pm at Hertfordshire Sports Village climbing wall.

02

Use the climbing wall safely

Follow the venue's entry, registration and safety requirements. The venue is responsible for access to its facilities.

03

Introduce yourself

Look for the group and say you are visiting Hertfordshire Climbing Club for the first time.

04

Enjoy the session

Climb at your own pace, meet people and see whether the regular club session feels right for you.

Before making a special journey

Regular plans can occasionally change. Once verified club contact or social details are available, they will be published here for session checks.

Frequently asked questions

Useful things to know.

When does the club meet?

The regular meet is usually every Thursday at around 8pm.

Where do you meet?

At the climbing wall at Hertfordshire Sports Village in Hertfordshire.

Do I need to contact the club first?

Verified online contact details are still being prepared. You can use this guide to plan a visit, but session arrangements can occasionally change.

What should I bring?

Bring what the climbing wall requires for your chosen activity and follow the venue's current guidance. Equipment hire and entry arrangements are managed by the venue.

Does the club provide instruction or supervision?

This website does not currently advertise instruction, coaching or supervision. Follow the climbing wall's safety rules and only take part in activities you are authorised and competent to undertake.

Is the group suitable for every level?

The club is described as a friendly local group, but verified details about membership, experience requirements and youth participation have not yet been published. Contact information will be added when available.
