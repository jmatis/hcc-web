About Hertfordshire Climbing Club

Climb together.

We are a friendly group of local climbers who enjoy good company, shared sessions and time on the wall.

Who we are

Friendly faces and a shared love of climbing.

The club is a relaxed place to climb alongside others, exchange encouragement and enjoy the social side of the sport. Our regular meet gives the group a simple way to catch up and get a session in together.
